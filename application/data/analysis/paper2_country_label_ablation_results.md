## Interpretation

Averaging providers within profile, changing only the label increased mean nitrogen by **+16.89 [+13.85, +19.97] kg/ha** and mean predicted adoption by **+0.343 [+0.267, +0.419]**. Yet the change in correlation with the observed Tanzanian farmer outcomes was **+0.032 [-0.073, +0.135]**. The label therefore shifted population-level predictions without reliably adding profile-specific agreement. In this experiment, country identity shifted the center of the simulated distribution more than it changed agreement with the observed ranking of Tanzanian profiles.
